# BUILD: the **7-day Claude build plan**

**One small build a day, 15 to 30 minutes each. By day 7 you've made 5 real things with Claude and shown one of them to a real person. No coding.**

## What you need
- A Claude account at claude.ai. The free plan works for most days; a paid plan gives you more room before you hit the limit.
- 15 to 30 minutes a day, and a notes app to tick off each day.
- For day 2 and day 7: one real local business near you with a weak website (a plumber, a salon, a café).

## How it works
- Every day has **one prompt**, a **"done when"** check, and an honest note.
- Copy the prompt, swap the [BRACKETS] for your own details, and paste it into Claude.
- The first answer is a draft. Reply with what's wrong ("the header's cramped", "shorter") two or three times. That's where it gets good.
- Missed a day? Carry on from where you are. The order matters more than the dates.

## Day 1 · Teach Claude your business (15 min)
Claude gives bland answers when it doesn't know who you are. Fix that once.

> Interview me to write a one-page brief about me and my work. Ask one question at a time (max 10): who I help, what I sell or want to sell, prices, tone of voice, words I never use, and what "good" looks like. Then write the brief in plain English under these headings: Who, Offer, Customers, Voice, Rules, Goals. Keep it under 400 words.

Save the brief. If your plan has **Projects**, make a project and paste it into the project knowledge. If not, paste it at the top of each new chat.

**Done when:** you have a saved one-page brief. *Want our template?* cutthecode.co.uk/brief

## Day 2 · Build a real website for one real business (30 min)

> You're a senior web designer for UK local businesses. Build a one-page, mobile-first website for [BUSINESS NAME], a [TRADE] in [TOWN]. Use only facts I give you: [PASTE their services, phone, area, opening hours, 2–3 real reviews]. Include a big call button, services, reviews, areas covered, hours and a simple quote form. Clean, fast, no stock-photo clichés. Make it a single HTML file.

Then reply twice with fixes: "make the call button follow me down the page", "the reviews should come first on phones".

**Done when:** it opens on your phone and you'd be happy for the owner to see it.
**Honest note:** don't invent reviews or claims. Only use what the business already says publicly. *The full 6-prompt version:* cutthecode.co.uk/prompts

## Day 3 · A quote calculator from a price list (20 min)

> Here's a price list: [PASTE IT]. Build a one-page quote calculator from it as a single HTML file. The customer ticks what they need and sees the total instantly. Show the maths under the total, line by line. Add a "send me this quote" button that opens an email with the quote filled in.

**Done when:** you've checked **3 test quotes by hand** and all three match. If one doesn't, tell Claude which and why, and check again.
**Honest note:** the calculator is only as right as the price list you give it.

## Day 4 · An inbox helper that drafts, you send (20 min)

> Here are 10 customer messages (names removed): [PASTE]. Sort them into: needs a reply today, can wait, spam. For each one that needs a reply, draft a short answer in the voice from my brief. Flag anything about money, complaints or refunds for me to answer myself. Don't promise anything I haven't told you is true.

**Done when:** you've edited and sent (yourself) at least 3 of the drafts.
**Honest note:** remove names, phone numbers and card details before you paste messages in. Claude drafts; you decide and press send. *More automations like this:* cutthecode.co.uk/agent

## Day 5 · Test an idea before you build it (20 min)

> I want to sell [IDEA] to [WHO] for [PRICE]. Play 10 different realistic customers from that group, each with a different budget and doubt. For each: would they buy, what's their main objection, and what would change their mind? Then give me: the 3 most common objections, the one question I should ask 5 real people this week, and the smallest version I could sell first.

**Done when:** you've written down the one question and sent it to at least 3 real people.
**Honest note:** these "customers" are Claude imagining people, not real demand. Use it to find the right questions, then ask real humans. Only real replies count.

## Day 6 · Turn your week into content (20 min)

> Here's what I built this week: [LIST days 1–5 in one line each]. Write 3 short video scripts (under 40 seconds each) about it. Each one opens with the result in the first sentence, shows one real step, says one honest catch, and ends with a one-word call to action. Then write one 6-slide carousel version of the best one.

**Done when:** you've posted one of them, or saved all three for this week.
**Honest note:** show real results only. If something flopped, that can be the best script. *How we turn scripts into videos:* cutthecode.co.uk/setup

## Day 7 · Show, don't pitch (15 min)
Most people try to sell an idea. Send the thing instead. Share your day-2 website (publish it from Claude, or drop the HTML file on a free host such as Netlify Drop), then:

> Write a short, honest message to [OWNER NAME] at [BUSINESS]. Say I made a free example website for them, link it, say there's no obligation, and ask if they'd like it set up properly. Under 80 words. Easy to say no. Don't send anything.

**Done when:** one real person has seen something you built.
**Honest note:** check UK email rules before you contact a business (the ICO's PECR guidance: limited companies can usually be emailed with an easy opt-out; sole traders usually need consent). A phone call or walking in is fine too. There's no promise anyone buys.
