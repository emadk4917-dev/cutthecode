Privacy notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This notice explains what personal information Cut The Code ("we", "us") collects, why, and your rights. We keep it short and plain.

Who we are

Cut The Code is the controller of your personal information. Contact: hello@cutthecode.co.uk. Postal address: [POSTAL ADDRESS].

What we collect

  • When you get the free pack or buy something: your email address, name or username (if you give one), what you got or bought, and when. Payments are handled by Whop; we never see your full card details.
  • From our emails: whether you open them or click links, so we can send things that are useful and stop sending things that aren't.
  • If you message us (email, Instagram DMs or comments): your message and username, so we can reply.

Why we use it

  • To send you what you asked for (the free pack, or a product you bought) and support you. Basis: our contract with you or steps you asked us to take.
  • To send you emails with prompts, builds and offers from Cut The Code. Basis: your consent or our legitimate interests in telling people who asked for our free pack about related products. You can unsubscribe in one click from any email, any time.
  • To understand what's working (for example, which emails get read). Basis: our legitimate interests.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for automated decisions that have legal or similar effects on you.

Who helps us (processors)

  • Whop: checkout, product access and payments
  • ActiveCampaign: our email list and emails
  • Zapier: moves your sign-up details from Whop to our email list
  • ManyChat and Meta (Instagram): Instagram messages and automatic replies
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website

Some of these companies are in the USA. Where your information is transferred outside the UK, it's protected by the safeguards UK law requires (such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge).

How long we keep it

We keep your email details while you're subscribed and for up to 12 months after you unsubscribe or stop engaging (an unsubscribe record is kept so we don't email you again). Purchase records are kept for 6 years for tax and accounting.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to or restrict how we use it, take a copy of it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@cutthecode.co.uk and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This website doesn't set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Whop's checkout uses its own cookies to run the checkout; see Whop's privacy policy for details.
